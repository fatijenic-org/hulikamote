# Clip checklist

Generated 2026-10-29 by `python -m tools.benchmark checklist`. Do not edit by hand: change `tools/benchmark/violations.py`.

Every violation needs real clips with known answers before it can be scored. For each clip you note WHEN each violation happens (see `benchmarks/README.md`).

**Safety first.** Use footage of violations that really happened, or stage them only on a closed or private road with permission. Never ask anyone to break traffic rules on a public road.

## Camera and clip rules (all violations)

- Fixed camera: no panning, zooming or hand-held footage (the zones and the learned flow assume one fixed view).
- Mounted above the road (about 3 to 8 m) looking down at 15 to 45 degrees, not straight down.
- Vehicles at least 120 px wide in the picture for plate, helmet and seatbelt checks (the plate check ignores vehicles under 60 px).
- At least 720p and 15 fps; 25-30 fps is better. Daylight for the first test set; evening and night as a separate set.
- Each vehicle visible for at least 3 s (the rules need several position updates; swerving needs 6 s).
- No heavy rain, lens dirt or strong backlight in the main test set.

Aim for at least **10 real events per violation** spread over at least 3 clips (different days or places), plus normal traffic in the same clips: the cars that do nothing wrong are what show false alarms. Add one clean clip (no violation at all) for every camera setup.

## Per violation

| violation | kind | a clip that SHOWS it | a clip that must NOT trigger it | camera view | wizard setup | limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `counterflow` | zone rule | A vehicle drives against the legal direction for 2 s or more. | Normal traffic on the same road; a vehicle crossing the road sideways. | Any fixed view where the road direction is clear. | Zones mode: draw the zone and the legal-flow arrow. Auto mode (flow_mode auto): no drawing, but the clip needs about 60 s of normal traffic first, because the legal direction is learned from it. | Two-way roads are never judged in auto mode (both directions are normal there). Needs a fixed camera. |
| `illegal_parking` | zone rule | A vehicle stands in the no-parking zone longer than the zone's time limit. | A vehicle that stops in the zone for less than the limit; moving traffic. | Any fixed view where the zone and the whole vehicle are visible. | Draw the zone. Set a short time limit (for example 10 s) so test clips stay short; the default is 180 s. | Judged on speed below 25 px/s, so a camera that shakes can hide a parked car. |
| `restricted_lane` | zone rule | A vehicle type that is not allowed (for example a car in a motorcycle lane) is inside the lane zone. | An allowed vehicle type in the same lane. | Any fixed view where the lane and the vehicle type are clear. | Draw the lane zone and tick the allowed vehicle classes. | Not in the thesis list; kept as an extra. |
| `pedestrian_lane` | zone rule | A vehicle stands still on the crosswalk for more than 3 s. | A vehicle that drives across the crosswalk without stopping. | Any fixed view where the crosswalk and the whole vehicle are visible. | Draw the crosswalk zone. | - |
| `illegal_turn` | zone rule | A vehicle turns into the forbidden direction (heading swings 45 degrees or more). | Vehicles going straight or turning the allowed way in the same zone. | Elevated view of the junction (the whole turn visible for 2 s or more). | Draw the zone and an arrow for the forbidden direction (an arrow pointing left = no left turn). | - |
| `illegal_u_turn` | zone rule | A vehicle turns back the way it came (150 degrees or more) and moves sideways at least 0.75 box widths. | A vehicle reversing in a straight line; ordinary turns. | Elevated view of the road section. | Draw the area where U-turns are not allowed. | The legal wording (local no-U-turn rules) still has to be confirmed before it is quoted on a ticket. |
| `swerving` | zone rule | A vehicle weaves left-right-left within 6 s (swings of at least half its width). | A single lane change; a steady straight path. | Rear or front view along the road; the vehicle visible for 6 s or more and travelling at least 2 box widths. | Draw the monitored zone (auto flow mode gives it the whole frame). | - |
| `red_light` | zone rule | A vehicle crosses the stop line while the light is red. | A vehicle crossing on green; a vehicle still in the junction when the light turns red. | View of the stop line and of the vehicles' wheels; the light itself visible if the real light is used. | Draw the zone, the stop line and either the light box or the simulated light (red start, red / green / yellow seconds). | An unknown light never produces a ticket. |
| `disregarding_signs` | zone rule | Breaking a sign: no entry, no turn, no stopping, vehicle ban or stop sign (one clip per sign type). | Obeying the same sign. | Fixed view of the sign's zone. | One zone per sign with its type (and an arrow or stop line where the type needs one). | - |
| `signal_light` | zone rule | A turn of 45 degrees or more without a blinking indicator. | Turns with a blinking indicator. | Close front or rear view where the indicators at the left and right edges are visible. | Draw the zone. | EXPERIMENTAL: it can over-flag (hand signals are lawful, motorcycles are exempt), so it is not a headline result. |
| `blocking_intersection` | zone rule | A vehicle stands still inside the junction box for more than 5 s. | A vehicle passing through, or waiting before the box. | Elevated view of the whole junction box. | Draw the junction box. | The legal basis (local anti-obstruction rules) still has to be confirmed. |
| `crossing_solid_line` | zone rule | A vehicle crosses the drawn solid line (it ends up clearly past it). | A vehicle wobbling on its own side of the line. | Fixed view where the line and the wheels are visible. | Draw the road section and the line. | The legal basis (no-overtaking rules) still has to be confirmed. |
| `illegal_loading` | zone rule | A bus, jeepney, tricycle or truck stands still in the no-loading zone for more than 8 s. | A bus passing through, a short stop, a car left standing (that is illegal parking), a vehicle outside the zone. | Fixed view where the zone and the whole vehicle are visible. | Draw the no-loading area; set the time limit and the vehicle types that may not load there. | Needs the vehicle class to settle (5+ looks). A jeepney is detected as a bus. The legal basis (MMDA / LGU loading rules) still has to be confirmed for Zamboanga City. |
| `truck_ban` | zone rule | A truck stays inside the banned road or lane zone for 1 s or more. | Cars, buses and motorcycles in the same zone; a truck outside the zone. | Fixed view where the zone and the vehicle type can be told apart. | Draw the banned road or lane; the banned types default to truck. | No clock: for a ban that applies only at certain hours, film those hours. The stock detector may call a pickup or van a car. The legal basis still has to be confirmed. |
| `tricycle_on_highway` | zone rule | A tricycle stays inside the highway zone for 1 s or more. | Motorcycles, cars and buses on the same highway; a tricycle on a street outside the zone. | Fixed view of the highway section where tricycles are banned. | Draw the highway section; the banned type defaults to tricycle. | Needs a detector that knows "tricycle" (the stock COCO model does not). Based on DILG MC 2020-036 / 2023-195; LGUs may allow some sections, so confirm the local rule. |
| `sidewalk_driving` | zone rule | A moving vehicle stays inside the sidewalk or bike-lane zone for 1.5 s or more. | A parked vehicle in the zone (that is illegal parking); a vehicle crossing to a driveway in under 1.5 s. | Fixed view where the sidewalk edge is clear. | Draw the sidewalk or bike lane. | No legal source was found yet: confirm the local ordinance before quoting it. |
| `double_parking` | zone rule | A vehicle stands in the travel lane beside another stopped vehicle (there first) for more than 8 s while a third vehicle drives through the zone. | A queue at a red light (nothing moves past); a vehicle stopped alone; a vehicle queued behind another. | Elevated or side view where the lane, the kerb vehicle and the passing traffic are all visible. | Draw the one travel lane next to the kerb, not the whole road. | Needs moving traffic inside the zone; the direction of that traffic decides what "beside" means. Experimental until it has run on real footage. |
| `overloading` | frame check | A motorcycle carries more riders than allowed (default 2) in most looks. | Motorcycles with one or two riders; pedestrians walking past a motorcycle. | Front, rear or side view with every rider's body visible and the motorcycle large enough to count heads. | Set the maximum riders (default 2). | Riders are counted from person boxes; heavy overlap hides riders. |
| `pickup_passengers` | frame check | A moving pickup or truck has people in its open back (bed). | A pickup with an empty bed; a pedestrian walking beside it; a parked pickup being loaded. | Front, rear or side view at a moderate height, so the bed and the cab are apart in the picture. | Set how many people are tolerated (default 0). | A straight top-down view cannot tell the bed from the cab. Pickups are usually detected as 'truck'. Legal basis: RA 4136 Sections 32 and 51 (the fine amounts are unconfirmed). |
| `riding_outside_vehicle` | frame check | People on the roof of, or hanging off, a moving bus, jeepney or truck. | People boarding a stopped bus; pedestrians beside a moving one. | The whole side or the rear of the vehicle at a moderate height. | None. | Someone on the rear step seen from straight behind is hidden by the vehicle. |
| `no_plate` | model check | A vehicle with no plate on it (rear view for motorcycles, which carry a rear plate only). | Vehicles with a plate; a motorcycle seen from the front. | Vehicles at least 60 px wide in the picture, plate side facing the camera. | None. Decided when the vehicle leaves the picture or the video ends. | A plate found at any fair look cancels the suspicion. |
| `no_helmet` | model check | A motorcycle rider without a helmet (or a helmet hung on the arm). | Riders with helmets; pedestrians. | Front or rear view of the motorcycle with the riders' heads visible. | None. | Needs the helmet model; the training data is not from Zamboanga. |
| `no_seatbelt` | model check | A car driver without a seatbelt, seen through a clear windshield. | Belted drivers; cars with tinted windshields (judged as 'no look' by design). | Front view of the car, close enough to see through the windshield, daylight. | None. | Tinted windows are skipped on purpose (safety gate); there is no tinted-window training data yet. |
| `distracted_driving` | model check | A driver holding a phone to the ear or at the wheel. | Drivers with empty hands. | Front view through a clear windshield, daylight. | None. | Not in the thesis list; kept as an extra. |
| `missing_side_mirror` | model check | A motorcycle with fewer than two side mirrors, seen from the front or the rear. | Motorcycles with both mirrors; any motorcycle seen from the side (one mirror is always hidden). | Front or rear view of the motorcycle with the mirrors large enough to see. | None. | Cannot work yet: the cabin model has no motorcycle-mirror training data (see datasets/CATALOG.md). |

## Where to get clips

- Your own camera, phone on a tripod, or footage from the barangay (with their permission and the Data Privacy Act in mind).
- Clips you already analysed in the Forensic Analyzer: `python -m tools.benchmark adopt <task id> <clip id>` copies the video and the wizard settings into the registry for you.
- Keep benchmark clips out of training: `python harvest.py protect <task id>` for clips that came from a forensic run.
