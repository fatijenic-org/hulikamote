The big picture

A dataset is a folder of pictures plus the correct answer for each one. We use four detection datasets (one for each YOLO model) and four helper collections (plate text, car look-alikes, a synthetic car-matching set and sharp plates for the picture enhancer). Almost everything comes from public sources with open licences, because no large labelled collection of Zamboanga traffic exists yet. That is the most important fact on this page: the models have learned what helmets, plates and windshields look like, but they have not yet been taught what our roads look like. The harvest loop (page 3) is how that gap is closed.

Honest reading of this page

Every score in this project so far was measured on the validation part of these public datasets, not on Zamboanga footage. The scores show that the models learned their pictures; they do not yet show accuracy on our roads. Page 3 explains how that is being closed, and page 4 states the status of each violation.

Dataset · trains the vehicle detector

Vehicle and rider set (primary_anchor)

Wide road pictures with a box around every motorcycle, car, bus, truck, tricycle and person. This is the set for the first-stage detector: the model that looks at every frame and finds the vehicles and the people on them. Pictures are 640 pixels on the longest side, the size this detector runs at. Today the system uses a stock COCO detector, which does not know tricycles or jeepneys. This set is how a Philippine detector would be fine-tuned.

How it contributes to the study

Every violation starts here: no vehicle box, no track, no violation. The person class is what makes overloading, pickup passengers, riding outside and the helmet crop possible, because riders are people standing on a motorcycle box. The tricycle class and jeepneys (as bus) are what the stock COCO model lacks, so this set is the path to detecting Philippine road users properly. A 6-class training run on it reached epoch 23 of 30 (page 2) and was not deployed.

Known limits

  • Class tricycle has only 27 boxes in val: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • Class tricycle has only 8 boxes in test: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • ph_vehicles: real Philippine jeepney / tricycle photos mixed with generic web, stock and Street View pictures; heavy augmentation (rotated, upside-down, noise)
  • ph_vehicles_selfdev: checked by eye 2026-10-25: Philippine web photos of single vehicles (jeepney, van, bus, tricycle), large boxes
  • The tricycle class is thin in validation and test, so its score is unreliable until our own footage is added (harvest, Vehicles tile).
  • Jeepneys share the class bus: the system cannot tell a jeepney from a bus.

How it contributes to the study

This set teaches the no-helmet violation. At run time the pipeline crops the motorcycle plus its riders (so heads are never cut off) and the model judges each rider; the ticket needs a vote over several looks. Training crops were cut the same way, so what the model sees in training matches what it sees in the system.

Known limits

  • Less than a quarter of the pictures are Philippine: collect and review Zamboanga footage (python harvest.py).
  • flashys_helmet: mostly Shutterstock / iStock stock photos (watermarks visible) of people on scooters, head-level boxes, augmented copies: weak evidence for real road accuracy
  • mapua_helmet: one street camera in Manila (night + day, 2022), whole 640x640 frames with heads of only ~15 x 26 px, no motorcycle / rider boxes to cut crops from; the helmet model is fed rider crops. Use after cutting rider crops with the vehicle detector. Only ~230 'No-Helmet' heads in 2,184 train pictures. Same camera as martinez_helmet
  • martinez_helmet: checked by eye 2026-10-25: Manila CCTV, 640x640 stretched frames; only ~105 'Not Wearing Helmet' boxes in train, so it mostly adds compliant riders from the target camera type
  • naratip_helmet: checked by eye 2026-10-25: head-level boxes on close-up photos of riders (some stock-like); the train split holds rotated copies with black corners; some pictures have only 'person' boxes (skate park, bicycles) and are skipped
  • Many pictures are close-ups or stock photos; CCTV-style views from a fixed camera are the minority. This is why the harvest loop matters for this model.

How it contributes to the study

This set teaches the plate finder. It feeds two things: the plate text on every ticket (find the plate, crop it, read it) and the no-plate violation (no plate found in any fair look). It is the weakest dataset for our purpose: none of its pictures is Philippine, and the largest part is grey Vietnamese barrier-camera footage. The plate model trained on it scores clearly below its target (page 2), and the plate model is the first one that should be retrained on our own harvested pictures.

Known limits

How it contributes to the study

This one model supports three violations: no seatbelt, phone use (distracted driving) and missing side mirror. The tinted_window class acts as a safety gate: if the windshield is tinted, nothing inside can be judged, so no seatbelt or phone ticket is made. The side-mirror class comes from one Philippine street camera and was found after the deployed cabin model was trained, so the deployed model has not learned mirrors yet.

Known limits

  • Class tinted_window has only 0 boxes in val: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • Class side_mirror has only 0 boxes in val: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • Class tinted_window has only 0 boxes in test: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • Class side_mirror has only 0 boxes in test: its score is unreliable (below 50).
  • Less than a quarter of the pictures are Philippine: collect and review Zamboanga footage (python harvest.py).
  • menna_cabin: enforcement-camera views through the windshield (Arabic plates). The same cars repeat across train / val / test (video frames + augmented copies): see the cleaning log. 'un seat-belt' is not mapped, absence = no seatbelt
  • govviolation_cabin: checked by eye 2026-10-25: Egyptian enforcement cameras (Arabic plates), the same kind of picture as menna_cabin
  • tint_windows: checked by eye 2026-10-25: the boxes are windows (not whole cars), but the pictures are general car-sale photos, many from INSIDE the car, and the set has no clear windows. A model trained on it can learn the picture style instead of the tint: do not trust the tint gate until it is tested on Zamboanga footage
  • mc_helmet_overload: checked by eye 2026-10-25: a street camera in the Philippines, one box per motorcycle, one per mirror seen ('sidemirror') and one where a mirror is missing ('no_sidemirror'). Only the mirrors that exist are trained: the rule 'fewer than two mirrors' counts them. Pictures are 640x360 so the crops are small
  • Validation and test contain no tinted_window and no side_mirror boxes, so those two classes have never been scored.
  • The cabin model deployed today was trained on 2026-10-13, before the mirror data was merged (2026-10-25).

Helper collection · trains the car matcher

Car look-alike sets (reid and reid_public)

The tracker must decide whether a car that reappears after being hidden is the same car. It does this with a fingerprint made by a small network (ReID). To improve that network we need folders of photos where each folder is one physical car.

Helper collection · teaches the picture enhancer

Sharp plate set (sr_plates)

Clear plate crops, cut from the labelled pictures of the plate set. A super-resolution model learns to rebuild a clear plate from a blurry one, so it needs clear plates to learn from; the trainer makes the blurry copies itself. A crop is kept only if it is big enough, plate-shaped, sharp and not a repeat, and it inherits the split of its picture so the enhancer is never scored on a plate it saw in training.

The data that matters most

Our own footage and the harvest inbox

Public data teaches what things look like. Only our own footage teaches what our cameras see. The project has one confirmed local clip so far (a rainy, wide-angle, 4K crossing in Zamboanga, analysis id 9db131ee). The harvest tool turns finished analyses into training material: it copies pictures the system was unsure about, shows them one at a time, a person checks or draws the boxes, and the accepted pictures join the dataset.

  1. Analyze a clip in the Forensic Analyzer (turn on Keep the uploaded video so frames can be sampled later).
  2. Collect: pictures are copied into training_lab/harvest_inbox/ with the model's suggested boxes.
  3. Check: one picture at a time; Enter = boxes are right, drag = draw one, N = nothing here, S = not sure.
  4. Accept: finished pictures are added to the model's dataset, only while that model is not training.
  5. Train and compare on the same protected benchmark clips.
Safety rules built into the harvest tool
  • It never writes inside hulikamote_backend/ and never uploads anything.
  • A picture is added only after a person has checked it.
  • Benchmark clips are protected and never harvested.
  • All pictures of one car stay on the same side of the train / validation split, and the test split is never written.
So nothing is hidden

Method

Rules that keep the numbers honest

RuleWhy it matters
One standard layout: images/{train,val,test} + labels/{train,val,test} + data.yamlEvery trainer and checker reads the same shape, so nothing depends on a source's own folder style.
A source's own train / valid / test split is kept (valid becomes val)Near-identical augmented copies never leak into the test part.
force_split: train for one fixed camera filmed over timeNeighbouring video frames in validation or test would make the score look better than it is.
The same photo in two splits: the train copy is quarantinedTest and validation stay as they are, so scores stay comparable. Nothing is deleted: files are moved to datasets/_quarantine/.
Near-duplicates are found with a perceptual hashCatches the same scene resized, re-encoded or slightly rotated. Unrelated pictures sit far apart.
Files named harvest__* (our reviewed CCTV pictures) are never moved, fixed or re-savedOur own checked work is protected from the automatic cleaner.
Country and 'Philippine' flags were checked by eye on picture sheets, not from project names'ph_plates' is Vietnamese and 'colorum_plates' is Indian / UK / US. A name is not evidence.

Terms used on this page

On screen, hover or tab to any dotted word for its meaning. This list is the same meanings, for the printed copy.

CC BY 4.0
Creative Commons licence: you may use and change the work freely if you credit the author.
CER
Character Error Rate: how many plate characters were read wrongly, as a share of all true characters.
class
One kind of thing a model can name, such as 'motorcycle' or 'license_plate'.
COCO
A famous public photo collection with 80 everyday object kinds (person, car, bus, truck...). Many models start from it.
dataset
A folder of pictures together with the correct answer (label) for every picture.
EasyOCR
A free OCR library. We use it to read the characters of a cropped licence plate.
fingerprint
A short list of numbers a network computes from a picture. Pictures of the same car should give close numbers.
GAN
Generative Adversarial Network: two networks compete, one draws images and one judges them, until the drawn images look real.
harvest
Our tool that collects pictures from finished analyses so a person can check them and add them to a dataset.
label
The correct answer written down for one picture. For us: a box around an object plus its class name.
MIT licence
A very permissive software licence: use freely, keep the copyright notice.
OCR
Optical Character Recognition: reading the letters and digits in a picture.
perceptual hash
A short fingerprint of how a picture looks. Pictures that look alike get fingerprints that are close.
Real-ESRGAN
A GAN-based super-resolution model that upscales pictures 4 times.
ReID
Re-identification: deciding whether two pictures show the same car.
safety gate
A rule that skips a check when the picture cannot support it, for example a tinted windshield that hides the driver.
super-resolution
Using a model to make a small, blurry picture bigger and sharper.
trust grade
Our own rating of a data source: A = peer-reviewed or official host, B = community upload with an open licence and a named author, C = unclear origin.
vote
Deciding from many looks instead of one: for example 'bad in at least 60% of the last 10 looks'.
VS Code
The code editor many developers use. The 'Open in VS Code' links jump straight to the line.
WER
Word Error Rate: the share of plates that were not read exactly right (here a whole plate is one 'word').
YOLO
'You Only Look Once'. A fast family of object detectors that find all objects in a single pass over the picture.
