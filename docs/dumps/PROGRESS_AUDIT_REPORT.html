Key Engineering Bottlenecks & Fixes

Race Condition

Tracker KeyError: 15 & CUDA 700 Crash

Unbounded default thread pool allowed asynchronous stream frames to race inside the shared tracking dictionary, corrupting memory inside the CUDA stream.

Solution Applied: Enforced a dedicated, single-worker thread pool (_PIPELINE_EXECUTOR) in stream.py to strictly serialize tracking updates.
Memory Saturation

1080p High-Resolution Video Lag

Full 1080p arrays (6.2 MB each) were copied, annotated, and JPEG-encoded on the CPU, causing frames to drop from 30 FPS down to 8 FPS.

Solution Applied: Implemented MAX_PROCESSING_HEIGHT = 720 cap immediately after frame capture. Decreased memory bandwidth by 56% while preserving full YOLO accuracy.
Detection Failure

License Plates Missed on Distant Vehicles

Full vehicle bounding boxes squashed to 320x320 reduced distant plates to unreadable 10px pixels; 15-frame intervals skipped quick passes.

Solution Applied: Focused crops specifically on the bottom 40% bumper zone, lowered confidence threshold to 0.35, and shortened interval to 5 frames.
Coordinate Drift

ROI Polygon Mismatch After Downscaling

Polygons drawn on 1080p native video frames failed to match vehicle centroids computed in the downscaled 720p internal array.

Solution Applied: Added _scale_polygon and _get_scaled_rois to dynamically scale polygon vertices to the working frame resolution at runtime.
